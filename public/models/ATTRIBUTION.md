# heart.glb attribution

Derived from the NIH Human Reference Atlas (HuBMAP), 3D Reference Organs for the Heart and Blood Vasculature, Male, v1.2 (Visible Human Male data from the U.S. National Library of Medicine), CC BY 4.0.

- Source files: `VH_M_Heart.glb` and `VH_M_Blood_Vasculature.glb`, https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases/tree/main/v1.2/models
- Portal: https://humanatlas.io
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Reference: Börner K, et al. Human BioMolecular Atlas Program (HuBMAP): 3D Human Reference Atlas construction and usage. Nat Methods 2025. PMID 40082611.

Changes made: kept the four chambers, interventricular septum, four valves, venae cavae, right brachiocephalic vein, right common and external iliac veins, pulmonary trunk and arteries, ascending and upper descending aorta and pulmonary veins; merged them into one file; welded and simplified the meshes (meshoptimizer, about 25% of the triangles); renamed the nodes. The catheter path, the distal right pulmonary artery branch, the internal jugular vein and the common femoral vein are drawn by the simulator and are not part of the HRA data. Generated by `tools/build-heart.mjs`.

# lungs.glb attribution

Derived from the NIH Human Reference Atlas (HuBMAP) 3D Reference Organ for the Lung, Male, v1.2 (Visible Human Male data from the U.S. National Library of Medicine), CC BY 4.0.

- Source file: `VH_M_Lung.glb`, https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases/tree/main/v1.2/models

Changes made: kept the bronchopulmonary segments of both lungs (no airways or cartilage), merged them into one mesh and simplified it to about 12,000 triangles (meshoptimizer). Generated by `tools/build-lungs.mjs`.
