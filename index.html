Tips

  • Inflate the balloon in the right atrium, then advance: the flow carries the tip across the tricuspid and pulmonary valves. Deflate before you pull back.
  • A wedge trace that is PA-like and higher than the PA diastolic is under-wedged (advance about 1 cm); one that climbs with no a or v waves is over-wedged (withdraw about 1 cm).
  • If the depth keeps rising with no new trace, the catheter is coiling: deflate, withdraw to about 20 cm and float again.
  • Each change of site is marked on the monitor with an orange dashed line. Compare the diastolic pressure before and after it.
  • Drag in the right panel to rotate the heart; scroll to zoom.
